Technical Exercise

RAD Developer — Dealing Room, Asia

Context

Our trading desk runs four cross-asset books out of Asia — rates, credit, FX and equity derivatives — across a range of currencies. Today the traders and risk managers monitor their positions, P&L and risk in spreadsheets that are fragile and do not scale well as activity grows.

We would like to see a working prototype of a proper Risk & P&L tool — something a trader and a risk manager could genuinely rely on in the morning, before the market opens.

What we are giving you

A set of extract files taken from our source systems. The accompanying README_datasets.txt describes every field.

FileContents
trades.csvThe current trade blotter across the four books
market_data.csvA month of daily market data
risk_sensitivities.csvSensitivities from the pricing library
fx_rates.csvA month of daily FX rates

These are real-world operational extracts. Please treat them as such.

What we are asking

Build a working tool that lets a trader and a risk manager understand the desk's positions, P&L and risk.

Please use Python for the backend and JavaScript or TypeScript for the front end. Every other choice is yours to make.

We are not looking for the longest feature list. We are looking for something we would trust on a live desk — and that a colleague who did not build it could pick up, run and extend.

Practicalities

  • You have five business days from receipt of this brief.
  • Deliver your work as a link to a Git repository (GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket) with its commit history visible.
  • Please do not commit the raw data files to the repository.
  • Be ready to walk us through your solution, and any part of it, in a follow-up conversation.
